The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog.  But why did this happen?  
Wouldn’t you think a fox, regardless of how quick he might be, would 
choose to run away from the dog?  Foxes don’t strike me as the type of 
animals to taunt other animals – especially predatory creatures capable of 
killing them.  I understand that foxes don’t have the mental capacity or 
reasoning ability of humans, but certainly they possess enough sense to 
flee from a potentially dangerous situation before disaster strikes.
